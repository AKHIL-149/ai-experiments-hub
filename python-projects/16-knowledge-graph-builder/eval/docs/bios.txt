Dr. Amara Osei studied physics at the University of Accra and completed her doctorate at ETH Zurich in 2009. She joined the Kestrel Institute in 2011. In 2015, Amara Osei became director of the Kestrel Institute's Quantum Lab, which she led until 2020. Her colleague Jonas Brandt has worked at the Kestrel Institute since 2013. Jonas Brandt is married to Elena Brandt, an architect from Lisbon. Elena Brandt designed the Harbor Library in Porto in 2018.
