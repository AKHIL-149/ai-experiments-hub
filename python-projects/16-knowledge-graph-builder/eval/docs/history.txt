The Treaty of Aldermoor was signed in 1648 in the city of Aldermoor by King Henric III of Vestland and Queen Sofia of Karelia. The treaty ended the Thirty Years of Salt, a conflict that began in 1618. Henric III ruled Vestland from 1631 until his death in 1659. His successor, Queen Margit, founded the University of Aldermoor in 1664. The university is located in Aldermoor and was named after the treaty.
