Nordwind Robotics was founded in 2012 by Ingrid Solheim in Bergen. The company builds warehouse robots and employs 340 people. In June 2019, Nordwind Robotics acquired Tessel AI, a vision-software startup based in Helsinki. Tessel AI had been created by Marcus Veld in 2016. After the acquisition, Marcus Veld became Chief Technology Officer of Nordwind Robotics, a role he held until 2023. In 2021, Nordwind Robotics reported revenue of 48M. Orbis Logistics, a shipping company headquartered in Rotterdam, signed a supply contract with Nordwind Robotics in 2022. Ingrid Solheim has served as chief executive of Nordwind Robotics since 2012.
