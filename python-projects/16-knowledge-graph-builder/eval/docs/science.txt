Professor Lena Hartmann at the Max Planck Institute discovered the Lumen effect in 2014. Her doctoral student Tomas Reyes extended the work at the Oslo Institute of Physics between 2016 and 2019. The Lumen effect was first reported in the journal Nature Photonics. Lena Hartmann has been a member of the German Academy of Sciences since 2017. In 2020, the Hartmann Prize was awarded to Lena Hartmann by the European Physical Society.
